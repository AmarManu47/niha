ABOUT LEGACY TAILOR

Legacy Tailor is a full-service alterations company that specializes in wedding, pageant and prom attire. We are the #1 choice of DFW brides and Miss America / Miss Texas beauty pageant contestants. In business for 20 plus years, the Legacy Tailor team has combined experience of well over 100 years. In addition to standard alterations of all types, we also offer complete and custom bead work and embellishments on all types of evening gowns, wedding dresses, and both men’s and women’s formal wear.
