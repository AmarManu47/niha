Welcome

Legacy Tailor is a full service alterations company that specializes in wedding, pageant and prom attire. We are the #1 choice of DFW brides and Miss America and Miss Texas beauty pageant contestants. In business for over 20 years, with our team’s combined experience exceeding 150 years, we offer complete and custom beadwork and embellishments on all types of evening gowns, wedding dresses, and both men’s and women’s formal wear.
